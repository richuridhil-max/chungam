CONSERVATION

Haute Joaillerie Care Protocol

Preserve the optical brilliance and metallurgical integrity of your heirloom.

01

Precious Metals & Gold Care

Clean 18K and 22K gold gently using lukewarm deionized water, mild non-abrasive soap, and a soft natural camel-hair brush. Avoid chemical dip cleansers.

02

Organic & Porous Gemstones

Emeralds, Pearls, and Opals require gentle ambient care. Never expose to ultrasonic cleaners or steam. Apply cosmetics and perfumes before adorning your jewellery.

03

Annual Maison Scintillation Audit

Our Place Vendôme and Geneva salons offer complimentary annual micro-prong audits, ultrasonic diamond bath treatments, and insurance appraisals.
